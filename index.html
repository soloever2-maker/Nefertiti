Meet your local host

Ciao, I'm Azza

I was born in Egypt, built a career in Saudi Arabia, and today I live in Italy. Twenty years of producing events taught me every detail that makes an experience unforgettable — but it was going on retreats myself that changed my life.

The further I lived from Egypt, the more clearly I saw what it holds: a sea that heals, a desert that silences the mind, and five thousand years of feminine power — from Nefertiti to the women leading circles today.

I didn't build Nefertiti because I wanted another business. I built it because retreats changed my life — and because I want to hold the door of my Egypt open for you and your community.

— Azza, Founder & Local Host
